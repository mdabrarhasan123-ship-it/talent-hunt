সেই জায়গা থেকেই শুরু হচ্ছে Talent Hunt 2026.

Talent Hunt 2026 is a creative talent discovery event organized by Dhaka College Photography Club for HSC 28 Batch students. Showcase your work in photography, filmmaking, graphic design, writing, motion design, UI/UX, generative art, 3D design and meme creation.
